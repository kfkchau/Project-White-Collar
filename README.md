# 🧠 Project White Collar Framework (PWCF)
**Governance-First AI Orchestration for Operational Integrity and Trust Optics**

---

## 🔍 What Is PWCF?
The **Project White Collar Framework (PWCF)** is a governance-first orchestration system for AI teams. It enables:
- **AI interoperability with human governance** — mirroring democratic principles like quorum rules, escalation paths, and layered verification.
- **Committee-driven trust optics** — visible governance mechanisms for executives and regulators.
- **Multilingual governance integrity** — ensuring semantic fidelity across global deployments.
- **Continuity safeguards** — fail-safes for leadership integrity and operational resilience.

---

## ✅ Why PWCF?
Current AI governance frameworks:
- Focus on **models**, not operational workflows.
- Lack **real-time enforcement** and **trust optics**.
PWCF solves these gaps by embedding governance inside orchestration:
- Immutable logs and decision trails.
- Committee-driven oversight.
- Escalation and override logic for critical risk scenarios.

---

## 🏛 Core Differentiators
- **Governance embedded in orchestration** → real-time accountability.
- **AI interoperability with human governance** → layered verification and committee oversight.
- **Scalability** → cohort-based design for parallel execution.
- **Multilingual adaptability** → governance integrity across languages.

---

## 🧩 Architecture Overview
PWCF is organised into **five layers**, each with specialised teams:
- **Translation Layer** — Input refining and output uplifting.
- **Analysis Layer** — Problem framing and research integration.
- **Tasking Layer** — Workflow design and activation logic.
- **Interaction Layer** — User-facing and system-facing operations.
- **Governance Layer** — Oversight, validation, escalation, and lifecycle control.
