# 🧠 Modular Agent Architecture – Design Overview  
**Purpose:** Define the layered ecosystem of bots and committees for governance-first orchestration. Built for **operational realism**, **trust optics**, and **multilingual adaptability**.

---

## 1. 🎯 Purpose  
This Framework supports complex, multi-layered work environments by decomposing responsibilities into discrete, auditable roles.  
Enhancements include:  
- **Layer-based architecture**.  
- **Committee-driven governance optics** for transparency and accountability.  
- **Operational continuity safeguards** and multilingual governance lifecycle.  

---

## 2. 🔍 Layers & Teams  
The architecture is organised into **five operational layers**, each with defined teams and roles:

### ✅ **Translation Layer**  
**Function:** Refines input and uplifts output for clarity and usability.  
**Teams:**  
- Input Refining Team: Linguistic Normaliser, Input Clarifier, Input Analyst, Input Lead.  
- Output Uplifting Team: Content Filterer, Section Organiser, Tone Stylist, Editorial Finisher, Output Lead.  

---

### ✅ **Analysis Layer**  
**Function:** Frames problems, maps requirements, and integrates research.  
**Teams:**  
- Business Team: Business Lead, Problem Framer, Solution Planner.  
- Research Team: Research Lead, Academic Searcher, Discipline Tagger, Research Integrator, Solution Generator.  

---

### ✅ **Tasking Layer**  
**Function:** Designs workflows and coordinates activation.  
**Teams:**  
- Tasking Team: Task Builder, Workflow Designer, Tasking Lead.  

---

### ✅ **Interaction Layer**  
**Function:** Manages user-facing and system-facing operations.  
**Teams:**  
- User-Facing Team: User Lead, First Respondent, Comms Strategist, Persona Tracker.  
- System-Facing Team: System Lead, Reader, Writer, Admin.  

---

### ✅ **Governance Layer**  
**Function:** Enforces compliance, validation, escalation, and lifecycle control.  
**Teams:**  
- Governance Team: Governance Lead, Escalator, Controller, Auditor, Verifier, Log Manager.  

---

## 3. ✅ Governance Hooks  
Governance is **embedded in orchestration**, not bolted on:  
- **Immutable Logs:** Audit Trail, Decision Log, Access Log (write-once).  
- **Committee Rules:**  
  - Accountability Committee: Governance Lead + Escalator + Controller.  
  - Record Committee: Auditor + Verifier + Governance Lead.  
  - Knowledge Committee: Tasking Lead + Escalator + Auditor.  
- **Escalation Logic:** Deadlocks → escalate to higher-level committee or user.  
- **Override Conditions:** Critical risk only; logged and owner-notified.  
- **Multilingual Governance:**  
  - Linguistic Normaliser translates default prompt.  
  - Verification chain: bot → Verifier → Team Lead → Accountability Committee.  
  - Logs and knowledge bodies maintained in user language mode.  

---

## 4. ✅ Operational Principles  
- **Governance embedded in orchestration** → real-time accountability.  
- **Transparency enforced to the teeth** → write-once logs, immutable records.  
- **Controlled autonomy** → bots operate under strict boundaries and lifecycle safeguards.  
- **Human-centric optics** → trust without complexity; committees visible and credible.  
- **Logic over opinion** → evidence-based reasoning, no value judgement.  
- **Universal applicability** → modular reasoning work across all white-collar domains.  

---

## 5. 🔗 Integration Points  
- **Knowledge Lifecycle:** Versioning, decay logic, multilingual fallback.  
- **Activation Protocols:** Ticketing + governance verification before execution.  
- **Conflict Resolution:** Arbitration, voting, escalation to user if deadlock persists.  

---

## 6. ✅ Bot Role Highlights
- **Governance Lead:** Chairs committees; enforces compliance.  
- **Escalator:** Detects systemic risks; initiates escalation.  
- **Controller:** Blindfolded lifecycle manager; activates/deactivates bots.  
- **Linguistic Normaliser:** Standardises input; builds linguistic profile; multilingual harmonisation.  
- **Persona Tracker:** Tracks user background, personality, and thinking patterns.  
- **Verifier:** Validates outputs; cross-verifies multilingual integrity.  

---

## 7. ✅ MECE Compliance  
- Roles are **mutually exclusive** (no overlaps).  
- Coverage is **collectively exhaustive** (all operational cases addressed).  
- Governance hooks and escalation paths uniquely assigned.
